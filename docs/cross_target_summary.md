## What moved and what did not

**EGFR (CHEMBL203)** — the target the whole pipeline was developed on. The refitted model beats the 1-NN baseline and beats the train mean (y std 1.3537), on 54 held-out scaffolds.
Docking enrichment — 1M17 / vina: AUC 0.632 [0.479, 0.769]; 1M17 / vinardo: AUC 0.734 [0.601, 0.852]; 8A27 / vina: AUC 0.593 [0.442, 0.739]; 8A27 / vinardo: AUC 0.510 [0.356, 0.655]. Admitted into the ranking objective: 1M17 / vinardo.
Generator yield from this target's most potent record: G1 rule 37/40, G3 SELFIES 40/40, G2 BRICS 40/40.
Applicability-domain gate on that output: 0.52 in domain, 0.29 borderline, 0.19 out of domain — out-of-domain molecules get no activity number in the answer.

**CDK2 (CHEMBL301)** — a second kinase, never used during development. The refitted model beats the 1-NN baseline and beats the train mean (y std 1.2807), on 53 held-out scaffolds.
No docking benchmark was run on this target, so no docking score is admitted into the ranking objective; the ranker degrades to the property-and-model objective and says so in the answer.

**thrombin (P00734)** — a serine protease: different fold, different chemotype. The refitted model does not beat the 1-NN baseline and beats the train mean (y std 1.5745), on 135 held-out scaffolds.

## Honest limits of this benchmark

- BRAF and BAX were requested as the third target. The ChEMBL web API returned HTTP 500 on every endpoint for the whole of this work, and no BRAF or BAX ligand set is in the local tool cache, so neither could be fitted. CDK2 (CHEMBL301) and thrombin (P00734) are the two targets for which measured ligand sets were already on disk; thrombin is the more informative of the two because it is not a kinase.
- The thrombin set is a two-mode selection (PubChem dose-response actives at pAct >= 7 and measured inactives at pAct <= 5, nothing between), so its absolute RMSE is not comparable with the ChEMBL sets. Its baselines are computed on the same split, which is what the comparison rests on.
- Docking was benchmarked only on EGFR and thrombin receptors. CDK2 has no receptor validation here, and the system's response to that is to refuse the docking score, not to use it unvalidated.
- A hit-probability calibrator exists for EGFR only. `P(>=1 hit)` is withheld on all three targets in published answers (see `docs/evaluation_report.md`, W2).
- Generator yield is *delivered / requested*, not a validity rate: the generators already discard unparseable structures internally, so a low yield means the generator could not find 40 distinct valid edits on that chemotype, which is the thing worth knowing.
